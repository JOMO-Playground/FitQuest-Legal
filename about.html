About FitQuest

Fitness should feel like progress, not administration.

FitQuest is being built in Lesotho around one product promise: the athlete should always understand the next worthwhile action and why it fits their path.

Our product principles

Luxury in simplicity

Premium does not mean filling every screen. It means deliberate hierarchy, strong feedback, consistent motion, resilient state, and information that earns its place.

Evidence before theatre

A foreground step sample is labelled as partial. A generated workout comes from a real library and passes local validation. A level changes from completed evidence. FitQuest does not manufacture certainty to make a graph look impressive.

Progressive by design

Workouts have a realistic structure—readiness, warm-up, main work, cooldown, and debrief. Difficulty can adapt from capability, effort, recovery, and past execution while preserving safety boundaries.

Local first, not local only

The core fitness database works on the device. Optional account, Coach, backup, and purchase features use external services only when configured and are described plainly in the Privacy Policy.

The current release boundary

FitQuest supports fitness and wellness. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent medical conditions and is not a substitute for a qualified healthcare or exercise professional.
