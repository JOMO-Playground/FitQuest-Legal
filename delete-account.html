Account control

Delete your FitQuest account.

Account deletion is permanent. It is separate from erasing the fitness database stored on a particular device.

Fastest: delete inside FitQuest

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Open Data & Privacy.
  3. Choose Delete account and device data.
  4. Read the impact, type the exact confirmation, and confirm.

The app authenticates the request, deletes owned remote backup objects where present, deletes the Supabase Auth account, disconnects subscription identity, and clears FitQuest-owned local data. Google Play subscriptions must still be cancelled in Google Play.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the same address used for the FitQuest account. Use the subject FitQuest account deletion request. We will verify control of the account before deletion and will never ask for your password.

Start deletion request

What is deleted

  • Supabase Auth account and active FitQuest sessions.
  • FitQuest cloud backup objects owned by that account, if the feature was enabled and used.
  • RevenueCat customer association where the integration is configured; legally required billing records may remain with Google Play or service providers.
  • App-owned local data and media when deletion is completed in the app.

What is not automatically deleted

  • A Google Play subscription or Play purchase history.
  • Data you previously exported or shared to another app or destination.
  • Service security, fraud-prevention, billing, or legal records that a provider must retain.
  • Local data on another device unless you erase it there or uninstall FitQuest.
Using FitQuest without an account?

There is no remote account to delete. Use Profile → Data & Privacy → Erase this device, or uninstall the app. Uninstall behaviour can be affected by Android backup settings.
