Privacy Policy

Effective 4 August 2026. This policy describes the FitQuest Android application and this website, including the account recovery, Coach, Health Connect, and active-GPS features enabled in the current testing release.

Privacy at a glance

Your core fitness database is stored on your device. FitQuest also offers optional account and network features. When you use one, the relevant data leaves the device for the specific purpose described below.

1. Who we are

“FitQuest,” “we,” and “us” refer to the developer and operator of the FitQuest application, based in the Kingdom of Lesotho. Privacy questions can be sent to fitquestsupp0rt@gmail.com.

2. Data kept on your device

Depending on what you enter or use, FitQuest stores profile and training preferences, goals, body measurements, routines, workout sessions and set evidence, readiness and effort responses, progression and achievements, steps, user-started GPS activity and route samples, Coach conversation history, exercise favourites, progress-photo files and metadata, app settings, and feedback drafts in app-owned storage.

Some of this information—particularly health, body, activity, route, and photo information—is sensitive. You decide what to enter and which permissions to grant.

3. Optional account and authentication

If you create or use an account, your email address, password verifier, account identifier, and session information are processed by Supabase Auth. The Android app stores its session using protected application storage. The website account portal stores its session in that browser until you sign out or clear site data. We do not receive your plaintext password.

If you choose Sign in with Google, Google authenticates you and provides Supabase Auth with the basic identity information needed to create or access your FitQuest account, such as your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image when available. FitQuest requests only the standard sign-in scopes needed for authentication; it does not request access to Gmail, contacts, Drive, or other Google account content.

4. Coach requests

When you are signed in, connected, and choose cloud Coach, your message and a bounded set of relevant fitness context are sent over an encrypted connection to a Supabase Edge Function. That function authenticates the request and sends it to a configured free text model through OpenRouter. Do not submit medical records or information you do not want processed by those services. Offline Coach guidance remains on the device and is labelled as offline.

5. Activity, sensors, and location

With permission, FitQuest reads only the Health Connect Steps record needed to show an all-day step total. Health Connect access is read-only. Health Connect or your device may continue collecting independently while FitQuest is closed; FitQuest refreshes the aggregate while open. A foreground Android sensor fallback may cover only the time FitQuest is open and is labelled as partial. The two sources are reconciled instead of added together.

FitQuest requests location only after you explicitly start an outdoor walk or run. During that active session, it uses location samples to calculate and save route evidence, distance, pace, speed, and elevation. If you grant background location and leave background continuation enabled, collection continues while FitQuest is minimized or the screen is locked and Android displays a persistent FitQuest notification. Collection stops when you pause, finish, or discard the session, disable background continuation, or Android force-stops the app. FitQuest never starts GPS on its own, does not collect location outside a user-started session, and does not use location for passive step tracking.

6. Photos and media

Profile and progress photos you choose are copied into app-owned device storage. FitQuest exercise guidance can use bundled reviewed images or fetch reviewed public-domain exercise images from a pinned GitHub-hosted repository. A remote image request necessarily reveals standard network information such as IP address and user agent to the host.

7. Cloud backup and the account portal

Cloud recovery is enabled in the current testing release for signed-in accounts. FitQuest can upload a JSON snapshot—including fitness records and any saved user-started GPS routes—to a private Supabase Storage path scoped to that account. A snapshot may be created after startup or when the app enters the background, but no more often than once every six hours. An established device is never silently replaced; a device without irreplaceable local evidence may restore the latest verified snapshot automatically after sign-in. Manual backup and restore controls are available in Settings.

After you sign in on the website, the My Progress portal can download your latest account-scoped snapshot into browser memory and show a read-only summary such as level, workouts, steps, goals, streak, and planned workout. The portal does not write to the app database or restore a phone. You may deliberately download the JSON snapshot. The pinned Supabase browser library is delivered by jsDelivr, which receives ordinary request information such as IP address and user agent.

8. Purchases

When paid subscriptions are enabled, Google Play processes payment and RevenueCat processes purchase and entitlement information, including an app user identifier, product, purchase status, and renewal/expiration state. FitQuest does not receive full payment-card details. Purchase controls remain unavailable in builds that have not passed store sandbox qualification.

9. Analytics and diagnostics

The initial release does not send product analytics or crash reports to an external analytics provider. Development logs are not a promise about a future build; if analytics or crash reporting is later enabled, this policy and the Play Data Safety form must be updated before release.

10. Why we use data

We use information to provide workouts, preserve sessions, calculate fitness estimates, adapt progression, show activity evidence, answer Coach requests you initiate, authenticate accounts, restore purchases, protect the service, respond to support, and comply with law. We do not sell personal data.

11. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it in FitQuest or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup behaviour outside our control. Auth data remains until the account is deleted. Optional backups remain until deleted with the account or under the applicable service-retention process. Service providers may retain limited security, billing, or legal records under their own obligations.

Use Profile → Settings → Data & Privacy to export or erase local data. Signed-in users can request account deletion there. You can also follow the public instructions on the Delete account page.

12. Security

We use platform application isolation, protected session storage, encrypted transport for network features, private storage policies, server-side authentication, and release scanning. No system is perfectly secure. Keep your device and email account protected and contact us if you believe your account is at risk.

13. Children

The initial release is intended for adults and is not directed to children. Do not create an account for a child or submit a child’s health or location data.

14. International processing and service providers

Supabase, Google authentication services, OpenRouter and the selected model provider, GitHub, Google Play, and RevenueCat may process data in countries outside Lesotho. Their contractual and legal safeguards apply to their processing.

15. Changes and contact

We will update the effective date and publish material changes before they apply. Contact fitquestsupp0rt@gmail.com with privacy requests.
