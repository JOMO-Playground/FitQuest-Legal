Data and media sources

Current for FitQuest 2.2.0, updated 4 August 2026. This register identifies third-party material shipped or fetched by the Android application.

Reviewed and pinned

Exercise media is resolved from a manifest pinned to a specific upstream revision. FitQuest does not display arbitrary search results or user-submitted exercise media.
