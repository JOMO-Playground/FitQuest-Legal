What do you need help with?

Include your Android version, FitQuest version, device model, and the action you were taking. Never send your password, recovery token, or full exported fitness data by email.

My password-reset link does not open FitQuest.

Open the link on the Android device where FitQuest is installed, then tap Open FitQuest on the recovery page. If the link expired, return to Sign in → Forgot password and request a new one. Do not reuse an old link.

Why do my steps change only while the app is open?

FitQuest clearly labels a foreground sensor fallback as partial. On compatible Android devices, the refined release can read an all-day aggregate from Health Connect after you grant read-only Steps access. The source and coverage are shown in Move.

Does FitQuest track my location all day?

No. Location is tied to a user-started GPS activity in the initial release. FitQuest does not use background location merely to count passive steps.

Can I use FitQuest without an account?

Yes. The local CORE Product works without an account. Sign-in is used for optional cloud Coach and other clearly labelled account features.

What can I do in the My Quest website portal?

After signing in, the portal can show a read-only summary of your newest private cloud snapshot and let you download that JSON file. It cannot start workouts or restore a phone. Use Profile → Backups in the Android app for device recovery.

How do I export or erase my data?

Open Profile → Data & Privacy. Local export uses Android’s share sheet. Local erasure and account deletion are separate, explicit actions.

Are subscriptions available?

Paid controls remain disabled until Google Play and RevenueCat sandbox qualification is complete. When enabled, the Play offer supplies the localized price and terms.

Is FitQuest medical advice?

No. Metrics and Coach guidance support fitness and wellness only. Seek a qualified professional for diagnosis, treatment, injury, pregnancy, medication, or other medical concerns.
